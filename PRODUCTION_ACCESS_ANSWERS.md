## About your closed test

**1. How did you recruit users for your closed test?**

> I used a paid testing provider (Testers Community), whose testers joined through a Google
> Group on the closed testing track. I also ran the app daily as the home screen on my own
> Google TV Streamer throughout the test.

**2. How easy was it to recruit testers for your app?**

> Easy

**3. Describe the engagement you received from testers during your closed test.**

> Testers installed the app on phones and TV devices and used it over 16 days: the app grid,
> weather, calendar and news cards, themes and Settings. They reported no crashes, and Play
> Console showed no policy or stability issues across four releases shipped during the test.

**4. Provide a summary of the feedback that you received from testers. Include how you collected the feedback.**

> Feedback came as a written report from the testing provider. Testers found no crashes or
> bugs. They asked for store screenshots that show the TV interface more clearly, a richer
> first-run walkthrough, and noted some lag while navigating on some devices.

## About your app

**5. Who is the intended audience for your app?**

> Owners of Google TV and Android TV devices who want a clean, ad-free home screen: their apps
> in one grid, with optional weather, calendar, news and smart-home cards. It suits people
> comfortable with a short one-time setup.

**6. Describe how your app provides value to users.**

> It replaces a home screen full of ads and recommendations with the user's own apps and
> glanceable cards: weather, calendar, news, now playing, air-conditioner and smart-home
> controls. No ads, no tracking, and all keys and settings stay on the device.

**7. How many installs do you expect your app to have in your first year?**

> 0 – 10K

(It is a paid niche utility that needs a manual setup step, so the lowest band is the honest one.)

## Your production readiness

**8. What changes did you make to your app based on what you learned during your closed test?**

> During the test I shipped four updates: setup from a phone via QR code so keys needn't be
> typed with a remote, Photo and Dark themes, a fix for the app grid drawing over other rows,
> see-through cards, and updated store screenshots and listing text.

**9. How did you decide that your app is ready for production?**

> It ran for 16 days with 12+ testers and as my own daily home screen with no crashes reported
> by testers or Play Console, and policy status shows no issues. The remaining tester requests
> are polish (walkthrough, screenshots), which I will address in follow-up updates.
